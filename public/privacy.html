Privacy Policy

Boing Battle store edition · Updated September 14, 2026

On your device

The game saves your chosen player name, language, sound preference and feedback cooldown locally. A player name is a nickname, not an account. You can change it in Profile. Clearing the app's data removes local settings.

Online play

Online matches send player nicknames, room codes and game events to our multiplayer servers and show nicknames to opponents. Connections expose technical information such as your IP address to hosting providers. Matchmaking and active rooms are maintained in server memory; hosting services may maintain operational connection logs.

Optional feedback

Sending feedback shares your message, category, nickname, optional email address, page address and browser/device information through FormSubmit for delivery to our support mailbox. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Feedback remains in the support mailbox while it is needed to handle your request. Contact us to request deletion of your message.

Service information: FormSubmit privacy policy. Website delivery and multiplayer hosting also process requests needed to provide those services.

Store edition

The store game does not require a cryptocurrency wallet or an energy transaction. AI play runs on the device. Online multiplayer and feedback require an internet connection.
